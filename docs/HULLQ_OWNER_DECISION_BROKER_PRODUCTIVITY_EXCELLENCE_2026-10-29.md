**Date:** 2026-10-29

## 1. Binding product direction

HullQ has **two equal customer-value pillars**, not a marketplace front end with an incidental broker admin panel:

1. **Buyer Excellence:** exceptionally broad, useful and rigorous Sail **and** Power discovery, well-designed listing/search/compare/contact journeys, reliable qualified technical matches and understandable evidence.
2. **Broker Excellence:** as much **genuinely valuable, high-quality daily work reduction** as is defensible and operationally sustainable: fast inventory operations, effective data reuse, onboarding and freshness, lead handling, automation, integration, team workflows and truthful performance insight.

The Owner explicitly approved this direction on 2026-10-29. Broker features are **not** to be postponed wholesale to "after the marketplace" nor implemented for feature counts alone. Customer task outcomes and correctness control both pillars.

HullQ is intended to be a **profit-oriented growing company** with quality as a competitive advantage. No artificial EUR 4,000–5,000 net/month or other founder-income cap constrains pricing, addressable-market decisions or roadmap. No particular income, subscriber count or growth rate is promised by this decision.

## 3. Professional work that HullQ should progressively eliminate

**Core workstreams**, tracked under [#400](https://github.com/StradaDelSole/HullQ/issues/400), include:

| Broker task | User-visible product outcome | Source / safety boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Create and update inventory | Minimum practical repeated data entry; templates, qualified model context, responsive editor and bulk changes | Model/design reference never silently becomes a claim about the physical yacht |
| Onboard and keep stock current | Broker-controlled, low-friction structured import; eventually a simple free authorized scheduled inbound feed, with visible synchronization/failure/stale states | No real external feed until accepted #355 intake/rights/worker/resource gates; 0083 import != ongoing synchronization |
| Publish to permitted destinations | Broker-controlled supported export, later licensed/contracted outbound destinations and withdrawal acknowledgements | 0088 seven exports != authorized upload to YachtWorld or other portals; #359 applies |
| Handle every lead | Reliable inquiry intake, assignment, reminders, follow-up and traceable outcome, later voluntary CRM connection | Consent, access segregation, privacy, no invented outcomes; no fee per ordinary buyer lead |
| Improve quality and measure results | Search-fit/missing-field diagnostics, accurate lifecycle, factual period reports and later qualified aggregate demand insights | Actual observations vs simulated demand; small-cohort privacy; no paid match/truth |
| Operate teams and offices | Coherent roles, reusable defaults, delegation and useful collaboration/automation | Server-side authorization and independent audit |
| Run Buyer + Broker workflows | Discoverable Search cards, Sail/Power technical depth, clean UI, working navigation and buyer contact linked to accurate broker records | Shared category/source truth; no ghost listings; not only backend unit-test success |

Feature breadth is an **ambition for ongoing high-quality development**, *not* a mandate to implement every speculative competitor feature or a fixed prelaunch count. The longlist of buyer filters is planning input and must be field-validated, proven useful and connected to real source-qualified inventory. Every advanced filter should have a sane broker capture/import path; every buyer-facing result must be actionable.

## 4. Value-based commercial boundaries

- **Free foundation:** truthful public listings, honest technical data and evidence, normal contact inquiries, elementary inventory control, authorized manual import/export and low-friction onboarding. For future external operations, an authorized **simple inbound scheduled feed** should be a free core capability with reasonable disclosed fair-use/resource limits, subject to #355 and rights.
- Advanced **automation, integration, collaboration and operational tools** may be paid when they genuinely save professional work or provide supported premium service levels. Price must reflect measured usefulness and sustainable operating economics, not sale of organic reach, truth status, normal leads or basic portability.
- Existing free core functions are **not silently converted to paid-only** later. Changes that would remove or materially restrict them require a new explicit Owner-reviewed policy and transparent customer terms; do not interpret this as unlimited free processing or support.
- Current example tariffs (including 49 / 89 / 159 EUR/month and broader ranges) are **research hypotheses only**, not an approved price table. Validate willingness to pay, premium package contents, user time savings, service costs and business sustainability through permitted broker conversations.
- Free private listings remain subject to proportional trader-status and anti-abuse checks. Free professional inventory is subject to reasonable technical/rights/fair-use protections, not a payment-driven organic ranking penalty.

## 5. Mandatory development and acceptance discipline

Every future buyer-search or broker-facing slice readiness/review **MUST reconcile** against this direction and its owning requirements, and explicitly answer:

1. **Job and benefit:** What repeated buyer/broker task becomes materially easier? Which existing user path and baseline demonstrate friction?
2. **Truth and completeness:** What current source/PhysicalBoat/BoatDesign truth and UNKNOWN/CONFLICT rules apply? Can usable listings be kept current without repeated re-entry?
3. **End-to-end proof:** Can a real browser with representative **synthetic** Sail+Power data complete the job, including missing/stale/negative/errors, not just pass a narrow domain test?
4. **Efficiency and operating cost:** What measurable steps, elapsed time, statement counts, sync staleness, support burden, or incident rates improve? What are the sustainable CPU/storage/network/third-party costs?
5. **Rights, resilience, parity:** Are permission/source rights, privacy, cancellation/retry/timeout, onboarding, access control, localization and joint Sail/Power applicability explicit? If a feature is category-specific, explain why.

The owning capability tracker must link implementation and exact-head independent review **only after** the slice is accepted. Marking a capability "implemented" from a plan, a demonstration page or research alone is prohibited. Competing commitments may be sequenced based on actual value and dependencies, but may not silently disappear.

## 7. Post-decision Claude review reconciliation — 2026-10-29 (planning clarification, not architectural approval)

Claude proposed moving a **declarative, reusable field/criterion architecture** ahead of large Search-filter waves. This is a strong **architecture candidate to be researched and explicitly selected**, not an already accepted implementation design or authorization to rewrite current `CriterionDefinition` / `FieldResolution`. Objective: minimize duplicate field semantics while retaining independent domain/source/rights constraints and Sail/Power specialist rigor.

A future design should evaluate **one versioned semantic field specification serving five user workflows where supported**: (1) buyer filter, (2) broker edit/claim, (3) inbound mapping, (4) authorized export projection, and (5) missing-data/Search-fit diagnostic. These are NOT always mechanically identical: actual PhysicalBoat claims vs BoatDesign references, differing format capabilities, rights and explicit UNKNOWN/CONFLICT, a manufacturer's as-designed option vs installed/refitted equipment, numeric unit conversions and per-destination loss/confirmation must remain separate. A shared catalog must not silently treat availability of a design value as proof for a particular boat, or advertise support for a field absent from a specific import/export profile. Before a full implementation, profile Search and verify the existing abstraction; assess migration/versioning, backward compatibility, adapter mapping, repeatable performance, evidence and browser coverage. Proposed sequencing: **measured Search safety/performance foundation; bounded semantic criterion/field contract; then accelerated multi-surface feature waves**, while delivering genuinely useful cards/navigation early rather than waiting for every data adapter.

**Exact code-scope correction:** `src/hullq/domain/import_nautix.py` admits currently accepted sail types (including `sail-motorsailer`) but no Power types. `src/hullq/domain/inventory_export.py` blocks resolved `POWER`, `MOTORSAILER` and `CONFLICT` for **NautiX** because the writer emits `sail-yacht`. Its **six table/JSON exports do not categorically reject Power**, but the 16-column `EXPORT_COLUMNS` projection lacks resolved vessel category and the accepted Power technical fields; do **not** assert Power roundtrip parity or complete Power exports. Sale and offer data may export in a restricted subset. Pursue exact category/format loss disclosure, not blanket claims of zero export capability.

**Broker evidence discipline:** count real completed imports, timely source updates, actually answered leads and supported outcomes. **Time saved is not directly observed by counting events**: any hours-saved view must show its measurement protocol, comparison baseline, assumptions and estimation label; do not fabricate earnings, buyer demand or response outcomes. Prefer a small number of repeatedly validated highest-value workflows and **integrate with established CRMs** rather than promising to build a full replacement. Simple authorized inward feeds are high-value onboarding; outbound portal sync requires an independently evidenced right and usable destination contract/profile for each target. No availability claim for YachtWorld or Boat24 integrations from file serialization alone.

**Classification:** current Sail/Power technical scope, NautiX format scope and structured export are `DECIDED_AND_IMPLEMENTED` within their bounded accepted slices; full Power import/export parity, cross-surface declarative schema and recurring inbound/outbound feeds are `DECIDED_NOT_YET_IMPLEMENTED` as high-level product directions or `GENUINELY_OPEN` in exact technical design, with trackers [#372](https://github.com/StradaDelSole/HullQ/issues/372), [#400](https://github.com/StradaDelSole/HullQ/issues/400), and [#355](https://github.com/StradaDelSole/HullQ/issues/355) and no automatic selected Slice. The proposed sequence is subject to a reviewed architecture plan and explicit Owner implementation selection.
