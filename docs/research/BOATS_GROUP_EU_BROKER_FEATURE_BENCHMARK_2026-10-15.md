## External platform restrictions and material negative findings

1. **Export format is not import permission.** BoatWizard inbound inventory feed approval is subject to Boats Group specialists and a separate agreement per customer; a partner cannot assume it may feed multiple brokers automatically. Source: https://support.boatwizard.com/article/635-data-import-feeds
2. **Feed source-of-truth is configurable, not universal.** In optional BoatWizard-Managed Feed mode the first imported listing becomes editable in BoatWizard, and subsequent externally resent changes to the same listing do not sync. Do not claim cross-platform bidirectional publication until the individual actual arrangement and acknowledgement is established. Source: https://support.boatwizard.com/article/785-boatwizard-managed-feed-listings
3. **Public Boats Group inventory API is read/access, not general broker listing write.** Source: https://api.boats.com/docs/overview and https://api.boats.com/docs/services/details?s=inventory
4. **EU co-brokerage display rights are restricted.** Their published EU terms require separating third-party co-brokerage from owned inventory; no scraping/copying/storing of licensed co-brokerage data by default. Marketing copy is not legal permission. Source: https://www.boatsgroup.com/terms-of-use-eu/
5. **NautiX 1.4 is a feasible export STANDARD, not a distribution contract.** It defines `adverts.xml` mandatory active record fields, stable advert/account identifiers, timestamps, statuses, omitted unavailable values, media order/checksums and optional push; output MUST validate against the pinned XSD. Source: https://www.nautic-network.org/documentation ; https://www.nautic-network.org/changelog ; accepted pinned XSD under `src/hullq/domain/nautix_schema/`.
6. **Writer format ≠ existing reader.** HullQ's `python-calamine` reads XLS/XLSX/XLSB/ODS, not writes them. XLSX writer `xlsxwriter` is already in dependencies; third-party XLS/XLSB/ODS writers demand security, CPython/Windows/Linux, true binary format and roundtrip compatibility proof. For XLSB a new `pyxlsbwriter` distribution exists but its current 0.1.x track is not acceptance/proof of security or production fitness. Source: https://github.com/tafia/calamine ; https://pypi.org/project/pyxlsbwriter/ ; https://github.com/pyexcel/pyexcel

## Decisions from this research

- **DECIDED_AND_IMPLEMENTED:** Organization-scoped broker draft and promoted inventory, current offer revisions/media and the accepted bounded eight-format import registry (0083).
- **DECIDED_NOT_YET_IMPLEMENTED:** REQ-BROKER-022/MVP-BROKER-009 export portability, standardized loss/field diagnostics and actual reliable writer validation; REQ-BROKER-028 periodic reporting and licensed integrations.
- **EXPLICITLY DEFERRED:** YachtWorld/BoatWizard submission, live external feed/refresh/withdrawal acknowledgements, third-party co-brokerage ingestion, buyer tracking without consent, made-up external market data/verification badges, sponsored placement in organic results.
- **GENUINELY OPEN:** A future platform-specific inbound file profile/partner contract, proof of stable legacy spreadsheet writers, future premium analytics/optimizer work and how much licensed off-platform event data (if any) becomes available.
- **CONFLICT_OR_REGRESSION:** Accepted PlanetScale PostgreSQL target (ADR-0012) supersedes an old DigitalOcean-only phrase still in `docs/governance/POST_0051_TRIGGER_GATES.md`; fix this bounded documentary drift in the 0088 readiness PR without provider re-selection. The current external-feed marketing copy never overrides EU co-brokerage legal rights.

This benchmark **does not** independently select or mark delivered new competitor features. It feeds post-0087 Owner-selected interoperability priority and remains explicitly distinct from the 0088 single export workflow.
