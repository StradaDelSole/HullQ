# Legal / Licensing Working Position

**Status:** Working project note, not legal advice.

Before commercial launch, obtain targeted Austrian/EU IP/IT advice if scraped Sailboatdata content would be used.

## Current working conclusions carried from project research

- Individual factual specifications are not automatically protected merely because they are facts.
- EU law also has a sui-generis database-right regime for qualifying database investment.
- Repeated systematic extraction of small portions can be relevant.
- Normalizing copied data does not retroactively create independent provenance.
- Attribution does not replace permission.
- Real-time meta-search is not automatically safe merely because external data is not permanently stored.
- Sailboatdata's published terms were previously identified as restricting automated access and commercial reuse without permission/license.
- Sailboatdata was identified as US-based in prior project research; the relevance of Article 11 of the EU Database Directive to eligibility should be checked professionally rather than assumed.

## Commercial paths

### A. License

If attractive, negotiate a narrow factual-data license covering machine-readable access, local storage, commercial use, normalization, own taxonomy, derived calculations, filtering/comparison, model pages, market matching, caching, updates and monetization.

Prefer excluding photos, drawings, brochures, editorial text, forums and third-party copyrighted material.

Given startup constraints, preferred economics are no large upfront fee, a pilot/startup period, deferred fixed fee, revenue share, or a small minimum plus revenue share.

### B. Independent database

Current preferred route: independently sourced production values with field-level provenance.

### C. Targeted opinion

If required, ask counsel specifically about a US database maker, Article 11 Database Directive, Austrian §§ 76c/76d UrhG, public factual data, website terms, scraping, UWG/unfair competition and clean-room strategy.
