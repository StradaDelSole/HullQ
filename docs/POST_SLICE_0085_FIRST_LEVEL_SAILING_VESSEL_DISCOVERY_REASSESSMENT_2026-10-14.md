# Post-SLICE-0085 Decision / Implementation Reconciliation — First-Level Sailing-Vessel Discovery

**Decision date:** 2026-10-14 (Europe/Vienna)  
**Status:** OWNER-SELECTED SLICE-0086; READY SUBJECT TO SEPARATE INDEPENDENT REVIEW AND MERGE  
**Canonical baseline:** `StradaDelSole/HullQ` `origin/main` `f2fd17168c348eaf821f938c551347775472b60d` (0085 independently accepted/closed and `FINISH_SLICE.bat` confirmed by Owner).  
**Owner decision:** HullQ is a **sail-powered vessel marketplace**, not a modern-monohull-only marketplace. Permit recreational sailing yachts, multihulls, motorsailers, traditional sail craft and legitimate commercially operated sailing vessels where accepted listing/trust/rights gates allow; launch emphasis remains recreational sailing yachts and cruising multihulls. Existence of a vessel type never proves commercial authorization or historical use. Buyer discovery requires a **first-level vessel-form/type experience BEFORE additional specialist dimensional filters**, built from orthogonal traits rather than a single mutually exclusive category.

## Owner confirmation — classification and evidence policy (2026-10-14)

**Status:** OWNER-ACCEPTED direction for the SLICE-0086 *readiness contract*, not implementation acceptance, READY review ACCEPT or authorization to run `START_SLICE.bat`. After review of competing listing platforms and the risk of false/inconsistent listings, the Owner confirmed the bounded physical-claim value sets below and required source-aware, non-inferential classification. This decision is captured here before any dependent implementation decision; production support remains `DECIDED_NOT_YET_IMPLEMENTED`.

| Independent optional PhysicalBoat claim | Exact `VALUE_ASSERTION` vocabulary | Supported public positive Search tokens |
|---|---|---|
| `physical_boat.hull_configuration` | `MONOHULL`, `CATAMARAN`, `TRIMARAN`, `OTHER` | `MONOHULL`, `CATAMARAN`, `TRIMARAN` |
| `physical_boat.cockpit_position` | `AFT`, `CENTER`, `FORWARD`, `MULTIPLE`, `OTHER` | `AFT`, `CENTER`, `FORWARD`, `MULTIPLE` |
| `physical_boat.deck_saloon` | `NONE`, `DECK_SALOON`, `PILOTHOUSE` | `DECK_SALOON`, `PILOTHOUSE` |

- Every new optional claim also supports assertion kind `UNKNOWN` with no value; omission of the entire claim wrapper is **distinct** from explicit `UNKNOWN`. No new `ABSENT` claim kind for these three. `OTHER` is an explicitly known *different* hull/cockpit configuration, **not** a placeholder for missing knowledge, and never proves a positive public choice. `NONE` means an explicitly known absence of the deck-saloon/pilothouse feature in the accepted single categorical field; it must not be inferred from a missing tag, not treated as `UNKNOWN`, and is **not a public filter in 0086**. These fixed enumerations follow existing v0.5/v0.6 BoatDesign `other` and v0.6 `none` vocabularies and the accepted concrete keel/rudder `OTHER` precedent; they do not make generic upstream labels trusted.
- Marketplace listings, importer mappings and broker/vendor tags are **source allegations, not verified construction facts**. Preserve the original tag, origin and import provenance without automatically promoting it to a current publisher `PhysicalBoatClaimSnapshot`, independently qualified BoatDesign/NamedVariant fact or public `CONFIRMED_MATCH`. A broker's explicit supported concrete assertion still needs the accepted revision/authorization path and current cross-Organization qualification; BoatDesign truth never automatically fills the physical claim. Contradiction, unsupported provenance or ambiguous category => `UNKNOWN`/non-confirmed search, not an invented positive match. Existing importer SLICE-0083 boundaries remain unchanged; do not add an automatic mapping pipeline in this slice.
- `CENTER` describes a demonstrably midships/central cockpit layout rather than a cockpit at the aft end of the vessel; **merely aft of the mainmast is insufficient** to distinguish it from `AFT`. `MULTIPLE` requires supported evidence for more than one **distinct actual cockpit**, not just helm/seating zones of a single cockpit marketed as a split cockpit. `FORWARD` similarly requires positively qualified configuration evidence. Where these distinctions are not supported, remain `UNKNOWN` rather than classifying from brand, aft-cabin description, exterior pictures or marketing shorthand.
- **Do not establish forbidden combinations from marketplace tags.** `CENTER + DECK_SALOON` is **not intrinsically contradictory**; the traits are orthogonal and each must be proven independently for the same applicable design/variant and current concrete boat. Likewise a claimed split cockpit is not automatic `MULTIPLE`. Accept a real inconsistency only when qualified physical/design evidence actually conflicts, never because a combination looks unusual.
- Current BoatDesign v0.6 `deck.deck_saloon` is a **single categorical value**, not a validated two-boolean model. `DECK_SALOON` and `PILOTHOUSE` must remain distinct and cannot be mechanically inferred from each other. If reliable descriptions genuinely support both in one boat, or sources use overlapping terminology without a defensible accepted single-value classification, retain the conflicting source descriptions and use non-confirmed/UNKNOWN under this 0086 field; a later separately approved saloon-versus-interior-helm two-axis model may resolve the overlap. Do not silently coerce either to `NONE` or equate a raised saloon with a verified internal helm.
- Public UX must obey `docs/PRODUCT_UX_PRINCIPLES.md`: show the actual applied hard criteria and evidence states, distinguish design/configuration from the **offered physical boat**, and never claim an all-inventory Search when the request is empty. It controls 0086 UI under `docs/PRODUCT_EXECUTION_PLAN.md` §Phase E.

## Selection and product result

**Selected next capability:** SLICE-0086 — **First-Level Sailing-Vessel Discovery: Hull Form, Cockpit and Deck/Shelter**. A buyer starts with clearly labeled sailing-vessel form choices and can combine them with the four accepted technical hard requirements. Broker inventory authors can state the concrete offered boat's relevant structure and privately preview the same predicates. This is ONE coherent bounded buyer-and-seller Search vertical, not arbitrary expansion of every taxonomy.

**Initial LIVE qualified public facets:** 
1. **Hull form** (`hull_configuration`): `MONOHULL`, `CATAMARAN`, `TRIMARAN` (one hard Search dimension). Accepted v0.5 and v0.6 BoatDesign configuration `hull_configuration` is directly semantically corresponding; where qualified `hull_count` independently contradicts it, the design field fails closed. `other`, `unknown`, missing/unqualified data are NOT positive requested categories. No inference from manufacturer/model name.
2. **Cockpit position** (`cockpit_position`): `AFT`, `CENTER`, `FORWARD`, `MULTIPLE` (one dimension). v0.6 `deck.cockpit_position` only, qualified; v0.5 **does not contain** the accepted deck field and remains UNKNOWN without an independently reviewed equivalent. An inferred deck layout from brochure photos or vessel silhouette is forbidden. `other`/`unknown` are not positive public request categories.
3. **Deck saloon / pilothouse form** (`deck_saloon`): exactly `DECK_SALOON` and `PILOTHOUSE` as **positive public hard choices** (one dimension). v0.6 `deck.deck_saloon` only; v0.5 missing is UNKNOWN. The v0.6 `none` is a genuine explicitly known absence, not the same as missing/UNKNOWN, and may be a separately asserted actual-boat claim, but is **NOT a public request value** in 0086. Keep Pilothouse and Deck Saloon distinct. Future negative `NONE` filtering needs separately reviewed semantics.

**Potential criterion count:** 4 accepted before SLICE-0086; 3 genuinely new accepted hard dimensions only if each is implemented and owner-accepted end-to-end; prospective count is **7**, never changed in READY or merely by private-preview additions. `deck_saloon` differs from `cockpit_position`; do not count enum alternatives separately. If not fully delivered, count advances by the verified implemented dimensions only after a separate explicit Owner decision on scope and acceptance.

**Vessel taxonomy coverage beyond this first bundle:** These are **independent conceptual axes**, not first-slice automatic hard categories:
- `hull_configuration`: one / two / three hulls, other and unknown kept in technical records;
- `sailing_propulsion_concept`: conventional sailing yacht, **motorsailer**, traditional sail vessel and specialist sailing craft need future explicit definition, complete BoatDesign+actual-boat source and registry vocabulary;
- `cockpit_position` and `deck_saloon`: the physically factual structural facets above, combinable (e.g. MONOHULL+CENTER+DECK_SALOON);
- `usage_history` vs `declared_intended_use` vs `commercial_certification`: independent truths. Registry `physical_boat.broad_use_history` is open-world DISPLAY_ONLY and includes `COMMERCIAL`, `CHARTER`, `SAILING_SCHOOL` etc.; it does NOT prove a vessel type, present use, certification, authority, compliance, title, charter readiness or licensing;
- `classic`/traditionally styled vessel is not proven by age, material, builder name or model popularity.

All sail-powered vessel types remain **eligible in principle** for marketplace participation when accepted supply/publication/rights gates pass; no new automatic seller exclusion based on unimplemented facets. No generic motorboats/unspecified powered craft product pivot and no special commercial ship compliance claim is authorized.
