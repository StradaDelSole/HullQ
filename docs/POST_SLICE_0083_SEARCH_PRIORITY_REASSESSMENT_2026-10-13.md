3. **Next prioritized candidate: rudder and skeg configuration.** Rudder position, support and balance are independent BoatDesign v0.6 dimensions, while the existing PhysicalBoat `rudder_configuration` vocabulary is more limited. Search mappings/claims must be explicitly reconciled, including `SKEG_HUNG` and other supported states, with no invented equivalence between rudder position, rudder support, or keel type. The Owner's earlier priority for rudder/skeg is binding as a product objective, **not** a preapproval of a merged criterion/taxonomy.
4. **Separately assess and close the existing keel-taxonomy gap:** current public `keel_configuration` intentionally excludes long/full keel even though the PhysicalBoat claim vocabulary includes `LONG_KEEL`; BoatDesign `full`, `modified_full`, `long_fin`, `bilge`, `swing`, etc. are not automatically equivalent to current public values. Any addition such as an explicit `LONG_KEEL` filter requires its own documented taxonomy/concrete-boat mapping, three-valued truth and regression review; it is a coverage extension of criterion #2, **not** criterion #3.
5. **Following expansion candidates** (in a later evidence-led order): beam, displacement, rig/sailplan and hull material; subsequently ballast, sail area, capacity/propulsion/accommodation measures and versioned derived metrics (for example capsize screening). These are existing technical-profile requirements or modeled fields, **not** already-supported public filters. Sequence and slice size must be based on actual FieldResolution, configuration applicability, PhysicalBoat claim coverage, test feasibility and user value. Do not claim every technical-profile field must be an immediate launch blocker or pack all fields into a single slice.
