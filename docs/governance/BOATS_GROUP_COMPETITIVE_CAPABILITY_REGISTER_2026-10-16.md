## B. Product execution / priority rule

**P0, already binding:** `REQ-BROKER-022`/0088 export and future recurring `REQ-BROKER-028` reporting before paid/broad broker launch. Runtime/auth/upload/retention pre-external intake risks (#355) and provider Production Readiness PASS are hard first-real-inventory boundary. **Do not insert unrelated features ahead of these accepted commitments.**

**P1 (next broker differentiators):** listing completeness/optimizer, verified broker trust, branded factual reports and eventually buyer Saved Search/Alerts and broker mobile/accessibility improvements. Only after verifying no already accepted equivalent functionality.

**P2 (sensible post-data features):** consented buyer intent, CRM delivery, volume-gated Search Demand, broker site catalog, voluntary owner referral, multi-authorized broker contact and safe phone/notifications. These may be useful without expensive external licenses but still require clear user consent/reliability boundaries.

**P3 (high-cost/regulatory/license-dependent):** market-comparables/soldboats, YachtCloser-style contracts, financing, cross-broker inventory licensing, outbound paid campaigns. Owner approval and legal/vendor terms first; possible alternatives are integration to existing providers rather than building whole financial/closing subsystems.

**REJECT-AS-IS:** paid ranking inside organic result classification, scraped competitor inventories/soldboat databases, unverified credentials/verification badges, cross-platform stalking-style intent collection, copied copyrighted media or using someone else's broker feed without permission.
