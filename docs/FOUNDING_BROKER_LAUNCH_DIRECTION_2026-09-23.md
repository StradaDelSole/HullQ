# HullQ — Founding Broker Launch Direction

**Date:** 2026-09-23  
**Status:** OWNER-DIRECTED STRATEGIC RECORD; exact commercial terms remain open  
**Scope:** broker acquisition / marketplace bootstrap / transparency  
**Does not modify:** active SLICE-0064 scope, accepted marketplace truth semantics, Search semantics, launch gates, pricing/entitlement implementation

## 1. Core launch problem

HullQ is a two-sided marketplace and therefore has an unavoidable bootstrap problem:

```text
insufficient inventory
→ weak buyer utility
→ weak lead volume
→ weak broker retention
→ insufficient inventory
```

HullQ should not hide this from early professional supply partners or pretend to have mature marketplace demand before it exists.

The preferred direction is explicit transparency:

- HullQ is new and has a real chicken-and-egg problem.
- HullQ must nevertheless offer brokers concrete technical and operational value from the start.
- Early brokers are being asked to take marketplace-stage risk and provide goodwill, inventory and feedback.
- HullQ should compensate that early risk with real economic advantages rather than vague promises.

Transparency must remain confident and factual. HullQ should not frame the offer as charity, ask brokers to support a small startup out of sympathy, or manufacture reach/lead claims.

## 2. Founding Broker Programme direction

Preferred working concept: **Founding Brokers**.

The exchange is intentionally reciprocal:

```text
Founding Broker contributes:
- early inventory
- product/workflow feedback
- tolerance for an early marketplace
- practical goodwill while network liquidity is still forming

HullQ contributes:
- low or zero initial economic risk
- materially easier/cheaper inventory workflow
- transparent performance evidence
- meaningful long-term founder advantage
- direct influence on the professional product while the workflow is still being shaped
```

Possible launch benefits include:

- free listings during the bootstrap period;
- free leads during the bootstrap period;
- no setup fee;
- no long lock-in;
- a durable Founding Broker commercial advantage once normal monetization begins.

These are strategic options, **not yet an accepted pricing schedule**. Exact duration, lead/listing thresholds, later price, eligibility and entitlement mechanics remain a future bounded commercial decision.

The underlying commercial principle is stronger than any particular “three months free” offer:

> Early brokers take the marketplace-stage risk; HullQ should initially carry the economic risk.

HullQ should avoid charging Founding Brokers merely because an arbitrary calendar period expired if HullQ has not yet demonstrated meaningful buyer value.

## 3. Broker value proposition

HullQ cannot win early professional supply by being “another place to list boats”.

The broker proposition must solve concrete current-platform pain and reduce operating friction. The target is a materially better professional workflow, including over time:

- the easiest practical path to create and maintain listings;
- avoiding duplicate manual entry wherever safe and legally/contractually permitted;
- import/feed/authorized source workflows so brokers do not need to retype inventory HullQ can ingest safely;
- explicit broker/publishing-Organization identity rather than unnecessary branding erasure;
- transparent listing performance and lead attribution;
- operational tools that are useful even before HullQ has incumbent-scale buyer traffic.

Exact external-source ingestion methods remain subject to rights, contracts, provenance and accepted HullQ data-governance boundaries.

## 4. Transparency to brokers

Early broker communication should say, in substance:

```text
HullQ is new.
We are solving specific problems brokers have with current platforms.
We are building technology intended to make inventory management and buyer matching easier and cheaper.
A new marketplace needs early inventory before buyer liquidity can compound.
We are therefore asking a limited group of early brokers to participate as Founding Brokers.
Because they take that early-stage risk, HullQ gives them meaningful economic advantages.
```

This is not a script and exact marketing language remains future work.

Hard communication rules:

- do not claim buyer reach HullQ does not yet have;
- do not fabricate or inflate impressions, enquiries or lead quality;
- distinguish observed marketplace facts from forecasts;
- tell brokers what HullQ can measure and what remains unknown;
- make commercial terms understandable rather than obscured by marketing language.

## 5. Performance transparency

A core professional differentiator should be factual visibility into the value HullQ actually creates.

Useful broker-facing evidence may include, once the required telemetry exists:

- listing views/exposure;
- search/query contexts that produced discovery where privacy-safe;
- technical criteria relevant to discovery;
- lead count and source;
- response/follow-up facts where supported;
- later source-to-outcome analytics where explicit outcome data exists.

This direction aligns with the accepted Broker Workspace Launch Gate and mandatory-capability work. It does not authorize premature analytics implementation in SLICE-0064.

## 6. Marketplace economics

HullQ should not be evaluated as though a stable €500/month micro-SaaS state were necessarily a meaningful success stage.

For a marketplace, low supply can be structurally unstable:

```text
very few brokers
→ very few listings
→ insufficient buyer utility
→ insufficient leads
→ broker churn
```

The useful question is therefore not “can HullQ make the first few hundred euros?” but whether it can cross a repeatable marketplace-liquidity threshold for a sufficiently valuable subset of buyer searches.

The project should measure this empirically rather than assign false-precision success probabilities before launch evidence exists.

## 7. Buyer-side implication

Broker acquisition alone is insufficient. Early supply becomes durable only if HullQ produces buyer utility and qualified enquiries.

HullQ should not compete as “a smaller YachtWorld with fewer listings”. Its intended buyer differentiation remains technical discovery and evidence-backed matching:

- structured technical Search;
- explicit UNKNOWN / insufficient-data semantics;
- design/configuration truth kept separate from concrete vessel/listing truth;
- factual Search evidence;
- explainable requirement sensitivity rather than opaque match scores.

The working marketplace thesis is that HullQ can become useful before it has incumbent-scale global inventory if it is unusually effective for a valuable subset of technical yacht-search intents.

## 8. Validation questions

The Founding Broker strategy should eventually be falsified or supported by measured evidence.

Key questions:

1. Can HullQ recruit the first 10, then 25, then 50 meaningful professional broker participants?
2. Will those brokers expose a substantial share of their relevant inventory rather than token listings?
3. Can HullQ onboard and maintain that inventory with materially less effort than duplicate manual entry?
4. Do buyers find relevant boats through HullQ that ordinary marketplace filtering makes harder to discover?
5. Does that demand create qualified broker leads?
6. Do brokers retain participation after the initial free/low-risk period?
7. Are brokers willing to pay once HullQ demonstrably creates operational or lead value?
8. Which Founding Broker benefit structure best preserves early supply without creating unsustainable permanent subsidy?

This record preserves the strategic discussion so it is not lost.

- change current SLICE-0064 implementation/review scope;

- set a fixed free period;

- declare the Broker Workspace Launch Gate PASS.
