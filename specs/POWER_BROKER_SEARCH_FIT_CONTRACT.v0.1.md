## User job and one capability

An authorized current PUBLISHER can open its existing private NativeListing inventory editor and test a bounded hypothetical POWER buyer criterion set: current drive arrangement alone, installed principal propulsion engine count alone, or the conjunction of both. The result must explain whether **this one offered PhysicalBoat** would satisfy precisely the same technical, qualified current-fact conditions accepted by Power Buyer Search, whether or not a canonical BoatDesign is linked.

Search-fit is illustrative technical eligibility, NOT real buyer demand, listing completeness, publication readiness, advertisement ranking, search volume, prospective leads, an endorsement, or HullQ independent technical verification. It makes **no writes** and never admits DRAFT into public Search.
