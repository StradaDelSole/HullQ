## One field, one meaning

`physical_boat.power_engine_count` means the **number of distinct installed principal propulsion engines/motors driving the offered PhysicalBoat**, **not** propeller count, shaft count, drive type, cylinders, generator sets, bow/stern thrusters, auxiliary/backup motors not part of principal propulsion, horsepower, installed electrical power, or the number of factory motorization options. Diesel, gasoline and electric principal propulsion motors are counted on the same factual basis; the energy/fuel type is NOT inferred. A series-hybrid generator is not counted again as a principal propulsion motor merely because it provides energy. If independent principal drive units cannot be established from the current publisher's qualified attestation, the field is UNKNOWN, not a guessed integer.

The field is a strictly typed **positive integer**, never a float/bool/string-coerced integer. The first bounded current-claim storage/authoring contract accepts **1–32**; values outside that envelope are rejected as unsupported, without truncation, clamping or pretending that 32 means “32 or more.” This is an initial input-support limit, not a factual claim that larger vessels do not exist. Historical snapshots may **omit** the field; omission and explicit UNKNOWN remain distinct. Old revisions retain exact replay/idempotency semantics.

The public Power-only hard-filter parameter is `power_engine_count` with exact canonical tokens **1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8**. This is ONE technical criterion even though its numeric equality is expressed as an existing `NumericLeafCriterion` RANGE with inclusive equal min/max; public tokens are exact positive decimal integer strings, no leading 0/plus sign, whitespace, exponent, decimal, sign, JSON-encoded number, empty value, or `9`/higher. **Current factual claims 9–32 remain valid and may support an evidenced non-match to a requested 1–8**, never a misleading affirmative. No `8+` bucket and no inequality/range/OR semantics in this slice.

Examples: `power_engine_count=2` means exactly two installed principal propulsion units, *not* “two motors of a particular manufacturer” nor “two propellers”. `power_drive=SHAFT&power_engine_count=2` means **both** hard conditions TRUE for the **same offered boat and the same applicable design configuration**, with publishing-Organization current facts from one coherent claim revision.

## Truth and sourcing

A design-unresolved, D29-current-public Power listing may become CONFIRMED_MATCH for count-only or count+drive only when the **publishing Organization's own current immutable PhysicalBoat claim revision** carries a positive VALUE_ASSERTION(POWER) and positive requested technical facts, all **from that same current revision**. A separate Organization, retired revision, catalog text, guessed engine brand, model marketing name, import label or design-level POWER alone cannot lend eligibility or a current installed engine count. Current contradictory other-Organization observations must fail closed. UNKNOWN, omitted, stale, cross-Organization conflict, invalid count, no applicable configuration or failed source read means INSUFFICIENT_DATA/typed error, not a false positive. D29, freshness and current public eligibility are unchanged. Count-only does **not** require a drive claim; count+drive requires both.

A qualified design-linked match requires genuine current BoatDesign/NamedVariant FieldResolution for the **specific exact count** at `/baseline/propulsion/engine_count` or `/overrides/propulsion/engine_count` (real v0.6 schema field, only where persisted/admitted FieldResolution exists) and a separately qualified current installed PhysicalBoat count. The same resolved NamedVariant/configuration must satisfy ALL requested leaves; “variant A has SHAFT while variant B has count=2” must **never** be a combined match. Design data can support only original factory count, not a current retrofit. Factory/count disagreement must not silently overwrite current state; without accepted evidence distinguishing applicable engine refit/motorization, classify insufficient rather than automatically matching/claiming confirmed conflict or a confirmed current fact.

For both design-linked and design-unresolved, publisher category + all technical claim observations must come from a **coherent PostgreSQL snapshot**, never multiple statements that can splice category, engine count or drive from incompatible committed revisions (SLICE-0093 Finding G). Existing field qualification/MUST-AND kernel, publisher source evidence, conflicting claim behavior and commercial-neutral Search result ordering apply. Failures in authorization, DB, source retrieval and transport are errors or typed nonconfirmed states, never success-empty fallbacks.
