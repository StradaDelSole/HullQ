## Proposed new-research stop strategy

Do **not** research all 2,172 raw builder strings.

Recommended sequence:

1. Southern Europe: 8–10 high-confidence yard candidates.
2. Taiwan / Hong Kong / Japan: 8–10 high-confidence physical builders.
3. Australia / New Zealand / South Africa: 8–10 high-confidence physical builders.
4. Re-count after every bounded batch.
5. Stop new universe research once the final deduplicated verified manufacturer/yard floor is safely above 120 **and** the country/macro-region/historical/archive floors are met.

Because the present recovered estimate is about 95–99, a pool of roughly 30 prioritized leads gives enough headroom for exclusions, brand-only outcomes and identity collapses without recreating the previous unbounded research run.

## Important exclusions / cautions during matching

The temporary index also contains entries such as `Various`, home-built/plans, combined builder strings, model brands, contract relationships and obvious spelling variants. These must never be interpreted as distinct yard entities by string count alone.

Examples of dangerous automatic inflation:

- one yard appearing under many spelling/country suffix variants;
- a brand and its physical contract builder both being counted without relationship modeling;
- a successor/renamed company being counted as a new physical yard solely because the corporate name changed;
- a designer or plans supplier being mistaken for a manufacturer;
- a one-off/custom builder being admitted without satisfying the slice's repeated/series production boundary.

The index therefore remains a **gap detector only**. HullQ's independent evidence policy and manufacturer/yard semantics remain authoritative.
