## 1. Kirié / Feeling

**Provisional decision:** `PROMOTE TO VERIFIED` as a historical series-sailboat manufacturer/yard; separate the Kirié yard identity from later Alliaura/Privilège ownership and from the Feeling brand.

- The recovered record already established the Feeling series and the Kirié physical shipyard but relied on one specialist secondary source.
- Independent French sailing-history material and Feeling-owner material consistently describe the Kirié family yard at Les Sables-d'Olonne, its transition into polyester construction, and the launch of the Feeling sailing-yacht range in the early 1980s.
- The 1986 Feeling 13.50 Route du Rhum production-boat result and the 1987 Feeling 10.90 Boat of the Year recognition are useful corroboration that this was a genuine repeated production line, not a one-off custom-builder case.
- Alliaura Marine took over Kirié in 2000 and continued the Feeling range. Later Privilège/Alliaura ownership should therefore be modeled as relationships/production continuation rather than silently extending the original Kirié legal/yard identity.
- Recommended production-era modeling for the **Kirié series-sailboat manufacturer role**: early 1980s to the 2000 Alliaura takeover, with the older 1912 general boatbuilding heritage preserved separately in ambiguity/history notes.
- The exact later date at which Feeling production finally ended under subsequent owners is not needed to verify Kirié itself and should remain a brand/owner-lineage question.

## 4. Victoire Jachtbouw

**Provisional decision:** `PROMOTE TO VERIFIED` as a historical Dutch manufacturer/yard, with a major correction to the recovered founder/start/end chronology and explicit separation of later restart entities.

- The recovered record's statement that Victoire Jachtbouw was founded in 1961 by Dick Koopmans Sr. and Frans Maas is not supportable.
- The Victoire owners' club documents the physical yard as **Jachtwerf Victoria**, founded in **1934 by Cor Vader** in Alkmaar, initially building canoes and wooden sailboats.
- In the late 1950s the yard moved into polyester construction; during the 1960s it became one of the early Dutch GRP sailing-yacht builders, beginning with the Victoire 22. Dick Koopmans was a principal **designer**, not the yard founder.
- The original Victoire Jachtbouw yard failed in September **2005**.
- A separate restart under **Victoire Yachts** followed in 2006 with Aemstel Ventures and Gilles Botenbouw and failed in June 2008.
- ShipRepublic then pursued Victoire 37/V42RE production/marketing from 2009 until 2017.
- Therefore the recovered single 1961–2018 record collapses multiple entities/continuations and must be corrected.
- Recommended modeling:
  - original Jachtwerf Victoria / Victoire Jachtbouw manufacturer-yard lineage: verified, 1934–2005 overall yard history, with GRP/series sailboat activity beginning in the late 1950s/1960s;
  - 2006 Victoire Yachts restart: distinct successor/restart relationship;
  - later ShipRepublic continuation: distinct production/brand relationship;
  - Dick Koopmans: designer relationship only, never silently promoted to founder/manufacturer identity.

Strong owner-association source:
- https://www.victoireclub.nl/werf
