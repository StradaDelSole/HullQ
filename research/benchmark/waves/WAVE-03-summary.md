# HullQ Controlled Benchmark — Research Wave 03

**Date:** 2026-08-20  
**Designs:** 8  
**Scope:** independent web research first; SailboatData only as post-hoc QA/reference comparison

## Research policy applied

1. HullQ evidence is gathered independently from manufacturer/builder, class/owners archives, manuals, specialist publications, brokers and community sources.
2. Source wording, generation/variant/option/state and measurement basis are retained.
3. Missing/conflicting facts remain unresolved rather than guessed.
4. SailboatData is checked only after the independent pass.
5. SailboatData values are not copied into HullQ evidence, summaries or fallback fields.
6. Reference crosscheck records only outcomes/anomaly classes such as match, partial, conflict, definition difference or not-found.

## B03-001 — Hallberg-Rassy 42E

**Independent sources**
- Hallberg-Rassy Club catalogue: https://hr-club.net/hr-catalogue/hr-42-e/
- Hallberg-Rassy manufacturer archive: https://www.hallberg-rassy.com/yachts/previous-models/hallberg-rassy-42e
- Wave Train technical review: https://wavetrain.net/2012/06/11/hallberg-rassy-42-a-classic-euro-cruiser/
- De Valk historical listings for individual-hull/configuration corroboration.

**Observed evidence**
- Enderlein/Rassy design, distinct from the later Frers 42F.
- Production 1980–1991; 255 boats reported by the HR catalogue.
- Hull length about 12.93 m; LWL about 10.50 m; beam about 3.78 m; standard/deep draft about 2.05 m; displacement about 11,500 kg; keel weight about 4,500 kg.
- Both ketch and sloop rigs existed, alongside deep and shoal keel configurations.
- Secondary/individual-hull material corroborates skeg-hung rudder language.

**Benchmark problem:** identity-critical `42E` suffix plus orthogonal rig and keel configuration axes. Individual-hull listings must not silently redefine the design baseline.

**Reference crosscheck:** no reliable direct record located during this pass.

## B03-002 — BENETEAU Oceanis 37

**Independent sources**
- BENETEAU heritage: https://www.beneteau.com/oceanis-2005-2014/oceanis-37
- De Valk and other historical broker records for individual-hull keel/rudder corroboration.

**Observed evidence**
- BENETEAU gives LOA 11.48 m, beam 3.92 m, lightship displacement 6,515 kg and air draft 16.65 m.
- Designer attribution: Jean-Marie Finot / Pascal Conq; Nauta interior.
- Independent hull records expose deep-keel draft and fin/spade-rudder details that are absent from the authoritative summary page.
- Individual-hull mass values differ from the builder's explicit `Lightship Displacement` and therefore require basis/scope handling.

**Benchmark problem:** primary page incompleteness plus individual-hull versus design-level mass semantics.

**Reference crosscheck:** partial; configuration/basis coverage differs. No reference values retained.

## B03-003 — Rustler 36

**Independent sources**
- Rustler Yachts keel-design article: https://www.rustleryachts.com/keel-design-explained/
- De Valk historical Rustler 36 listing for numeric corroboration.

**Observed evidence**
- Rustler explicitly describes a traditional long keel with cutaway forefoot and a **keel-hung rudder**.
- A representative individual-hull record gives LOA 10.78 m, beam 3.35 m, draft 1.75 m, displacement 7.5 t and ballast 3.6 t.

**Benchmark problem:** highly authoritative appendage taxonomy versus hull-specific numeric evidence must remain separate evidence classes.

**Reference crosscheck:** taxonomy conflict/partial match. Reference wording is less compatible with the builder's explicit keel-hung statement; no reference values retained.

## B03-004 — Seafarer 26 (McCurdy & Rhodes generation)

**Independent sources**
- Good Old Boat technical article: https://goodoldboat.com/seafarer-26/
- Good Old Boat comparison: https://goodoldboat.com/seafarer-26-2/

**Observed evidence**
- This Seafarer 26 was built 1977–1985 and designed by McCurdy & Rhodes; an earlier different Seafarer 26 was designed by Philip Rhodes.
- Fin keel; displacement 4,600 lb.
- Rudder described specifically as hung on a **partial skeg with a bottom bearing**.
- Historical material describes substantial solid laminate and an integral lead-filled keel.

**Benchmark problem:** reused model name plus explicit partial-skeg evidence from a defunct-builder context.

**Reference crosscheck:** strong ordinary-field agreement but taxonomy loses the more precise partial-skeg relationship.

## B03-005 — Southerly 110

**Independent sources**
- Owner's manual mirror: https://manualzz.com/doc/28420109/southerly-110-yacht-owners-manual
- De Valk historical technical listings.

**Observed evidence**
- Rob Humphreys design / Northshore context.
- Representative geometry: LOA about 10.82 m, LWL 9.22 m, beam 3.57 m, draft range about 0.71–2.18 m.
- Owner's manual explicitly states **twin rudders as standard**.
- Technical material describes hydraulic swing keel, fixed grounding plate and protective centerline skeg relationship.
- Published mass/ballast observations differ among hull-specific and secondary records.

**Benchmark problem:** swing keel state, fixed grounding structure, twin-rudder count and protective-skeg relationship are separate facts; mass differences require scope/basis review.

**Reference crosscheck:** partial; geometry broadly compatible but appendage relationship is incomplete and mass conflict remains unresolved.

## B03-006 — Contessa 32

- The design remains a live/new-build lineage using the same hull/deck mould concept.

- Class rules are authoritative constraints/measurement artifacts, not automatically nominal production values.

**Benchmark problem:** current-new-build values versus historical design values need explicit era/applicability semantics.

**Reference crosscheck:** partial/era difference; no reference values retained.

## B03-008 — Moody 33 Mk I / Mk II

**Independent sources**
- Moody Owners archive Mk I: https://www.moodyowners.org/Moody_Archives/17_boat.htm
- Moody Owners archive Mk II: https://www.moodyowners.org/Moody_Archives/18_boat.htm
- Technical discussion: https://www.moodyowners.info/threads/moody-33-mk1-vs-mk2.22269/

**Observed evidence**
- Mk I and Mk II have explicit production periods/counts in the owners archive and share broadly the same principal dimensions.
- Mk II changes concentrate on accommodation/cockpit rather than an obviously new underwater hull.
- The archive/discussion exposes multiple sail-area definitions: rating/foretriangle-style area versus actual larger headsail + main area.

**Benchmark problem:** an apparent generation sail-area conflict can actually be a measurement-definition conflict.

**Reference crosscheck:** partial/definition-compatible. No reference sail-area value retained.

# Wave 03 findings

1. Partial skeg must remain distinct from generic skeg support.
2. Era applicability matters at design level.
3. Rig and keel axes can be orthogonal.
4. Swing-keel is not a complete appendage model.
5. Builder chronology may conflict with a reference database.
6. Sail-area conflicts may be definition conflicts rather than rig changes.
7. Reference taxonomies can be less precise than independent evidence.
8. Individual-hull listings are useful but scope-limited.

After Waves 01–03, **25 designs** had been actively re-researched. The benchmark remained research evidence, not production canonical data, and did not authorize broad ingestion or PostgreSQL persistence.
