## SEED-21 — Seafarer 26 (McCurdy & Rhodes generation)

**Candidate identity:** 1977–1985 Seafarer 26 designed by McCurdy & Rhodes; an earlier different Seafarer 26 was designed by Philip Rhodes.  
**Secondary technical source:** Good Old Boat.  
**URL:** https://goodoldboat.com/seafarer-26/

### Observed facts

- Built 1977–1985 by Seafarer Yachts, after an earlier different Seafarer 26 design.
- Fin keel.
- Rudder explicitly described as hung on a **partial skeg** with a bottom bearing.
- Displacement 4,600 lb.
- Source provides detailed laminate/construction observations and notes the builder is defunct.

### Why this is difficult/useful

- Fills the explicit `partial skeg` edge case missing from the first 20 designs.
- Reused model name refers to a different earlier design, proving that manufacturer + model string alone cannot establish BoatDesign identity.
- Defunct builder means primary manufacturer data is not generally available; strong specialist secondary evidence and archival leads matter.
- Rudder/skeg classification is present in prose rather than a tidy specification table.

### Likely automation

- Basic dimensions/specification extraction from article text: medium.
- Generation disambiguation and partial-skeg taxonomy: review recommended.
- Production values should be corroborated with original manufacturer/designer documentation where available before canonical acceptance.
